Why Prism

Tests check the cases someone thought of. With Prism you state what your app must never do and must always do. Lean then proves each statement for every possible request.

Kai Developer new to Prism

“What is Prism, and what would it do for my code?”

Explanation: read it end to end, away from the keyboard.

The app ships as one sealed file. Anyone can check that file before they run it.

Proved and tested are different claims

Proved means the Lean kernel checked a property for every request. Tested means the examples replayed correctly.

Rules, examples and the reading of requests are tested, not proved. Nothing proves that your rules are the right rules. That judgment stays with you.

Part of the appProved or testedHow it is checked
Property (never or always)ProvedA theorem the Lean kernel checks for every request
Rule (when, then)TestedThe examples, and the properties that constrain it
ExampleTestedReplayed byte for byte as an acceptance vector
Reading a requestTestedMalformed requests answer the reserved invalid
The app fileCheckedIts BLAKE3 fingerprint is recomputed on load

Where Prism fits

Use Prism when a wrong answer is costly. Good first apps are approvals, eligibility, limits, access rules, and checks an agent relies on.

Today the guided path builds decision apps within these limits:

PartLimit today
RequestOne line of up to 8 fields, separated by spaces
FieldA whole number, or a choice of 2 to 32 words
AnswerOne of a fixed set of outcomes, plus the reserved invalid
RulesUp to 24, tried in order; the first match decides
ConditionsCompare a field with a value or another field: is, is not, <, <=, >, >=

Conditions do no arithmetic. A leap year app cannot test whether a year divides by 4. Pass the result in as a field instead, such as divisible-by-4: yes or no.

Lean kernel
The small checker at the heart of the Lean prover. It accepts a proof only if every step follows the rules.
theorem
A statement about the model that the Lean kernel has checked, so it holds for every input, not only the ones tested.
property
What the app must never or always answer. Each one becomes a theorem the Lean kernel proves for every request.
